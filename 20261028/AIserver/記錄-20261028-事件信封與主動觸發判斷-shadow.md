```json

  "importance": 0.8,
  "dedupe_key": "hko:rain",
  "cooldown_s": 1800,
  "status": "shadow",
  "meta": {}

```

- 落盤：`runtime/events/external_events.jsonl`（append-only）

- `sync_from_collectors(limit)`：把 `runtime/collect/items.jsonl` 最近事件轉信封，只記冷卻通過的
- CLI：`python agents/event_frame.py sync|latest`
